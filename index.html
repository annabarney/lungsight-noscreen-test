Volunteer for the LungSight study.

LungSight is a research initiative working to develop a low-cost, easy-to-use tool to help detect chronic lung disease early, potentially before symptoms become noticeable.

The aim is to use a smartphone, tablet or home computer to gather information about breathing, speech, and facial and upper body movements. By volunteering, you can help us develop this home-based screening tool.
